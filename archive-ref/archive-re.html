Welcome to the Reel Asian Digital Community Archive!

This is a community archive, please navigate with sensitivity and mindfulness. Users must adhere to the unique copyright and sharing permissions on each of the entries. Sharing of material against the wishes of the copyright holder’s discretion is strictly prohibited.

If there is an entry that involves you or your likeness in the archive that you want removed or edited, please reach out to specialprojects@reelasian.com to have it addressed.

This is not a complete directory of all films and events from Reel Asian, but rather pieces of it. We focused on things submitted from community members, and material available online and in our own archive to release.

If you just want to explore: Browse using the filtering options, and see what you find!

Or, type in a key word, place or name in the search bar.

Here you will find:

  • Memorabilia and keepsakes of community members at Reel Asian over the years
  • Several commissioned and programmed films available online, searchable via tags, names and key words
  • Archived panels and talks hosted through our industry series
  • Archived Internal, Operational and Marketing Reel Asian documents and items
  • Critical writing on programmed films/panels: Youth Critics Initiative Pieces (TACLA)
  • Wee Asian arts activities over the last 5 years
  • And more!

Wander around, and have fun!

How to use the archive

THE SEARCH BAR

The search bar searches the title of an item as well as its description. This is a good place to look up names of people, programs, places, and countries. It searches live as you type, so sometimes there may not be anything tagged exactly for what you are looking for (ie. “Thailand”) but there are entries for something similar (ie. “Thai”). Consider different terms and keywords to use to find what you are looking for.

FILTERS

These filters are a great place to start exploring. These filters are divided into 6 sections:

  • Medium (What type of format is the entry about? Short film, feature film, Event)
  • Programme (Explore Reel Asian’s different programming subsections like: Community Archive Submission, RA:X, Reel Ideas)
  • Tags (There are over 100 tags in this database, they range from topic, genre, description of event and more)
  • Year (Filter by a specific festival year)
  • Language (Language of video or text based piece)
  • Country (Country of film production)

HOT KEYS

ENTER will automatically search what you have typed in the search bar

ESC will automatically minimize the full screen search page
